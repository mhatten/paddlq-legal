What is PaddlQ?

PaddlQ is a pickleball match scheduling app used by a group of players. The PaddlQ organizer uses the app to organize matches and notify players by text message.

To opt in, text JOIN to the PaddlQ phone number provided by your organizer.

You may receive the PaddlQ number and JOIN instructions from an organizer through their own non-PaddlQ communication channel, such as in-person conversation, personal text message, email, or an existing group chat. PaddlQ does not send text messages to you until you first text JOIN to PaddlQ.

By texting JOIN to PaddlQ, you agree to receive SMS messages from PaddlQ including pickleball match invitations, game reminders, player confirmations, cancellations, and player coordination.

Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity. You may receive up to a few messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any PaddlQ message. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

For help, reply HELP to any PaddlQ message.

PaddlQ does not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number, SMS consent, or opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
