What is PaddlQ?

PaddlQ is a pickleball match scheduling app used by a group of players. The PaddlQ administrator uses the app to organize matches, and notify players via text message.

By providing your phone number to PaddlQ, you agree to receive text messages from PaddlQ including match scheduling invitations, game reminders, and player confirmations.

Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity. You may receive up to a few messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any PaddlQ message. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

For help, reply HELP to any PaddlQ message.

PaddlQ does not sell, rent, or share your phone number with any third party.
