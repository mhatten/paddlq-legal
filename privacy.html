PaddlQ – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

1. Overview

PaddlQ is a personal pickleball match scheduling application that uses SMS to coordinate games among a private group of players. This Privacy Policy explains how PaddlQ collects, uses, and protects your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service: your phone number and first name. We do not collect email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used solely to send SMS notifications from PaddlQ related to match scheduling invitations, court availability alerts, game reminders, and player confirmations.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on match scheduling activity. You may receive up to a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

5. We Do Not Share Your Information

PaddlQ does not sell, rent, trade, or share your phone number or any personal information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Your mobile number will never be shared with outside parties.

6. How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any PaddlQ message to opt out at any time. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

7. Data Retention

Your phone number is retained only while you are an active participant. Upon request, your information will be permanently deleted.

8. Contact

For questions, contact the PaddlQ administrator directly through your existing group communication channels.
